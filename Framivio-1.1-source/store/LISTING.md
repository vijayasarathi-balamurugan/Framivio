# Draft Play Store listing — review after device testing

App name: Framivio

Short description:

Resize photos and videos for social posts. Create locally, share anywhere.

Full description:

One creation. Every platform.

Framivio is a pocket-sized editing studio for fitting your photos and videos to different social formats. Choose a canvas, adjust the framing, add your own text watermark and export a fresh file. Your originals stay untouched.

PHOTO TOOLS

- Ready-to-use canvas sizes for social posts, stories, thumbnails and more.
- Fit the whole image or crop to fill the frame.
- Blur, color and transparent backgrounds.
- Drag positioning, zoom, rotate and flip.
- Text watermarks with position, color, size and opacity controls.
- JPG, PNG and WebP exports.
- Re-encoded photos without copied EXIF or GPS metadata.

VIDEO TOOLS

- Reframe a supported video with black-background Fit or centered Crop.
- Trim the beginning and end, mute audio and add a text watermark.
- Export MP4 video on your Android device.

MADE FOR YOUR WORKFLOW

- Favorite presets and save custom canvas sizes.
- Export multiple selected photos with shared settings.
- Browse your finished exports and share to installed apps.
- Light and dark workspaces, with reduced-motion support.

LOCAL BY DESIGN

Photo and video processing happens on your device. No Framivio account or media upload is required. Core editing works offline. When enabled, ads and Google Play purchases need internet access.

Important details: Android 10 or later. Large imported photos are downsampled for memory safety. Video inputs are limited to 512 MB and 10 minutes; output dimensions are even numbers up to 1920 px per side. Codec and HDR support depend on your device. Keep Framivio open during video export. Safe-zone guides are approximate and are not exported. Removing metadata does not remove private information visible or audible in media. This app is independent of the social platforms named in its presets.

[Include the following only for a configured monetized release: Free editing is supported by occasional post-export ads. Framivio Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase for ad removal, batch photo exports and custom presets. Prices and availability are shown by Google Play. No subscription.]

Owner note: Do not claim AI subject detection, video blur backgrounds, background rendering or a guaranteed compression ratio. Those features are not implemented. Publish only capabilities that pass your device tests.
