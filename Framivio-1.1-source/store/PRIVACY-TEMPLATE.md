## Media processing

Framivio processes the photos and videos you select on your device. We do not operate a media-upload server, require a Framivio account, or transmit your selected media to our own servers.

Selected source files are copied into the app's temporary storage for editing. You can clear imported files in Settings. Photo exports are re-encoded and do not copy source EXIF/GPS metadata. This does not redact information that is visible in the image or audible in a video.

Completed exports are saved in app storage and in your device gallery. Removing an export inside Framivio removes the app copy, not the separate gallery copy. Uninstalling clears app storage; gallery copies remain until you delete them through your device's gallery/files app.

When you share an exported file, you choose the receiving app, which handles it under that app's own terms and privacy practices.

## Preferences and purchases

The app stores appearance preferences, favorites and custom presets locally. In a monetized build, it may store a signed Google Play purchase record locally to remember Pro access offline. Google Play processes the purchase; Framivio does not receive your payment-card information.

## Permissions

Framivio uses the Android system file picker to access media you explicitly choose and the system media store to save new exports. It does not request broad access to your entire photo library. Internet/network access supports enabled Google services. It does not request contacts, microphone recording or location access.
